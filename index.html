Fakhro

A private tool for restreaming live broadcasts

Fakhro is a small private tool run by one person for their own use. When a live broadcast it follows starts, it sends that broadcast to a YouTube channel the owner manages, and it can schedule and end the live streams on that channel through the YouTube API.

It is not a public service: nobody can sign up, and it only acts on channels whose owner has signed in to it themselves.
